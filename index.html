Features

Everything an academy needs to run training and follow athlete development.

Athlete Profiles

Each athlete gets a profile that keeps their details and training information in one place.

Sports Programme Selection

Athletes browse the available programmes and choose the one that fits their goals.

Training Schedules

Coaches create and manage schedules and allocate athletes to batches.

Attendance Tracking

Record who attended each session and keep a clear attendance history for every batch.

Performance Records

Maintain structured performance records so results can be compared over time.

Progress Dashboard

Dashboards with search and filtering help coaches and athletes follow progress at a glance.

About Us

The Sports Academy Management System is a college project that brings the daily work of a sports academy into one platform. It links athlete profiles with programme selection, training schedules, attendance, performance records and progress tracking, so athletes and coaches work from the same information.

The system is being developed in stages, starting with the front end.
